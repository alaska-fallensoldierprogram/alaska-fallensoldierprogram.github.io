The Fallen Soldier Program

Honoring the fallen with dignity, respect, and duty.

An all-volunteer, employee-led program at Alaska Airlines dedicated to the respectful transport and honor of fallen U.S. service members and their families.

★★★ ★★★
Our Story

Duty, Honor, Country

The Fallen Soldier Program began in May 2011, started by Alaska Airlines employees after ramp technicians witnessed the remains of a fallen service member handled without the dignity they deserved. In response, an all-volunteer group of employees came together to make sure it never happened again.

The result was the Fallen Soldier Cart — a specially built, flag-draped cart used to transport the remains of deceased military personnel to and from the aircraft, following military protocol every step of the way. Each cart carries the insignia of the six branches of the U.S. Armed Forces: Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, and Coast Guard.

What started as one cart has grown into a program with a simple, lasting goal: to give every fallen service member the honor they earned, at every airport Alaska Airlines serves — and to make the carts available to any airline that needs them.
